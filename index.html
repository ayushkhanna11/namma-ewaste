What We Do

Solving Bengaluru's E-Waste Bottleneck

Bengaluru produces over 100,000 tonnes of e-waste annually. Individual doorstep pickups are often logistically inefficient and costly.

Namma E-Waste allows households and office units to log discarded electronics. Once a hub reaches a minimum threshold (60kg for societies, 100kg for tech parks), an automated collection alert dispatches an authorized van to collect the accumulated waste in a single trip.
